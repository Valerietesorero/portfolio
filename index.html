We start by agreeing on the responsibilities, priorities, working hours, and communication rhythm. Support can focus on ongoing executive operations or a defined project, depending on what your business needs.

I’m based in the Philippines and have supported teams across Australia, the US, the UK, and Europe. We agree on overlap hours and deadlines before starting, with written updates for work completed outside your day.

Can you work inside the tools we already use?+

Yes. I work with Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, project management tools, CRMs, accounting systems, and construction platforms. We’ll review your existing setup and confirm any training or access needed.

How I Can Help
Your first 90 days
with me on board.

Bringing on an EA shouldn't add work to your plate. In month one I take over the day-to-day. By the end of month three you have documented processes, live trackers, and automations that keep running without anyone chasing them.

  1. 30
    Days 1–30

    Learn and take over

    • Take over calendar, inbox triage, and follow-ups from week one
    • Audit recurring tasks, tools, and where time is being lost
    • Map how your key processes run today
    • Set up a shared task tracker and a weekly check-in
    You get: a current-state process map and a ranked list of what to fix first.
  2. 60
    Days 31–60

    Build and standardize

    • Write SOPs for the most frequent and most error-prone tasks
    • Clean up CRM records, shared drives, and file naming
    • Build trackers for projects, invoices, and hiring
    • Automate the first handoffs, like form to CRM to notification
    You get: an SOP library, organized files, and live trackers your team can use.
  3. 90
    Days 61–90

    Automate and hand over

    • Automate repeat workflows with Make or Zapier
    • Launch a weekly dashboard with the numbers you care about
    • Walk your team through the SOPs so nothing depends on one person
    • Run a 90-day review: what changed and what's next
    You get: a weekly executive dashboard and a plan for the next quarter.
